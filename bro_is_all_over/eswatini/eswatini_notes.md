## Faits sourcés (résumé)
Royaume d'Eswatini (ex-Swaziland, rebaptisé 19 avril 2018), 17 364 km², ≈1,2 M hab., enclavé entre Afrique du Sud et Mozambique ; siSwati + anglais ; lilangeni = rand 1:1 ; Mbabane (administrative), Lobamba (royale/législative). Roi Mswati III depuis 1986 ; dernier monarque absolu d'Afrique ; le roi nomme le premier ministre (HRW).
Interdits (conseils officiels, sans citer le Canada dans la voix, l'image, les SRT ni la description) : pas de conduite de nuit, éviter minibus khumbi, éviter manifestations, photo interdite (bâtiments gouvernementaux, militaires, résidences royales), insulte au roi = longues peines.
Lieux : Mlilwane, Hlane, Mkhaya, Sibebe, Malolotja (canopy 10 tyroliennes, pont 50 m), Grand Usutu (rafting), Mantenga (sibhaca), Ngwenya Glass, Swazi Candles, Lion Cavern (mine d'ocre ≈48 000 ans, Nature Communications 2024).
Écarts de sources : Lion Cavern 41-43 000 ans (anciennes datations) vs ≈48 000 (2024) ; Sibebe formulation prudente.

## Script THE DON'T LIST (35,9 s)
- D0: Five things you must never do in Eswahteeni.
- D1: Don't drive at night. Cattle wander onto dark roads, and armed carjackings have hit main routes.
- D2: Don't ride the khumbi minibus taxis. They are often poorly maintained and overloaded.
- D3: Don't join protests, even peaceful ones. Security forces have used force, and some protests turned violent.
- D4: Don't photograph royal residences, soldiers, or government buildings. It is prohibited without permission.
- D5: Don't insult the king. Offensive words or actions toward the royal family can bring prosecution and long prison terms.

## Script THE MUST DO LIST (39,4 s)
- M0: Five things you must do in Eswahteeni.
- M1: Do ride the Maloloja canopy tour. Ten zip lines and a fifty-meter suspension bridge cross a mountain gorge.
- M2: Do raft the Great Oosootoo River. Guides take you through mostly grade three rapids in a remote stretch.
- M3: Do watch the seebahkah dance at Mantengah Cultural Village. Dancers kick high and drum twice a day beside beehive huts.
- M4: Do track a black rhino on foot at Mkahyah. Guides run the walks, and odds are among Africa's best.
- M5: Do sleep in a beehive hut at Mleelwahneh. The restaurant there overlooks a hippo pool.
