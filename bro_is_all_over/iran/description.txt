Twenty-four hundred years ago, people in the Iranian desert were making ice in summer, with no electricity. In this video: the yakhchal (ice pit), the badgir (Persian breeze catcher) and the qanat, the underground tunnel that powers both, and why Iran is now short of water.

Travel note: Canada currently advises Canadians to avoid all travel to Iran (travel.gc.ca, last updated 13 October 2026). This video is a heritage story, not travel advice.

Facts: yakhchal dating is debated (popular accounts say about 400 BCE; the oldest surviving buildings are younger). Tehran reservoir levels are from November 2025 reports (about 10 to 11 percent full on average).
