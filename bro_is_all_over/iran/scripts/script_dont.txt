Five things you must never do in Iran.
One. Don't photograph government buildings, police, or military sites. It's banned, drones included.
Two. Don't count on cards. It's a cash economy, and ATMs are for local banks only.
Three. Don't wear shorts. The dress code is strictly enforced, for men and women.
Four. Don't carry alcohol or drugs. The penalties are severe.
Five. Don't believe the first "it's free." That's taarof. A driver may refuse payment two or three times, and still expect it.
