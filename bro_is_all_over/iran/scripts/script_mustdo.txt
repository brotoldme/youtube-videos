Five things you must do in Iran, when it's safe again.
One. Stand in Naqsh-e Jahan Square, in Isfahan. A UNESCO site, built around sixteen hundred.
Two. Walk through Persepolis, the ceremonial capital of the Achaemenid Empire.
Three. See the Pink Mosque, in Shiraz, at sunrise.
Four. Look up inside the wind catcher at Dowlat Abad Garden, in Yazd. Almost thirty-four meters tall.
Five. Eat faloodeh in Shiraz. Frozen noodles, rose water, and lime. One of the world's oldest frozen desserts.
