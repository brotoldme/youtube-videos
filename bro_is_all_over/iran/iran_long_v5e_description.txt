Twenty-five hundred years ago, one empire ruled almost half the people on Earth, and its ceremonial capital stood in Iran. Here are eight reasons to visit: Persepolis and Chogha Zanbil, Isfahan, Shiraz, Nowruz, the food, the extremes, Yazd and its qanat, and the people.
